Immob24 for Zoom

Documentation · Last updated: 2026-06-29

The Immob24 integration for Zoom lets real-estate agents schedule, launch, and follow up on client meetings directly from their CRM. This page explains how to add, use, and remove the integration.

What the integration does: creates Zoom meetings from an Immob24 lead or property; sends meeting invites to the buyer/seller; stores the meeting link on the lead's timeline; posts a short summary back to the CRM after the meeting ends.

Requirements

  • An active Immob24 account (any paid plan or a running trial)
  • A Zoom account (Basic, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Education) with permission to install Marketplace apps
  • An organization administrator role in Immob24 (Agency Admin or Super Admin) to install the app the first time

1. Adding the Immob24 app to your Zoom account

  1. Sign in to Immob24 at https://immob24.com/login.
  2. Open Settings → Integrations from the left sidebar.
  3. Find the Zoom tile and click Connect.
  4. You will be redirected to Zoom and asked to sign in with your Zoom account.
  5. Review the requested permissions on Zoom's consent screen and click Allow.
  6. Zoom sends you back to Immob24. When you see "Zoom connected", the integration is ready to use.

Permissions Immob24 requests

  • meeting:write — create meetings on your behalf
  • meeting:read — read the meetings Immob24 creates
  • user:read — read your Zoom profile (name, email, time zone) so invites look right
  • webinar:write (optional) — only requested if your Zoom plan supports it and you enable webinars

Immob24 never reads meetings, chats, or recordings that it did not create itself.

2. Using the integration

Schedule a Zoom meeting from a lead

  1. Open any lead in Immob24 (Leads tab → click a lead).
  2. Click Schedule meeting on the lead's toolbar.
  3. Pick a date, time, and duration. Optionally add a property from your listings.
  4. Choose Zoom as the meeting platform and click Create.
  5. Immob24 creates the Zoom meeting, saves the join link on the lead's timeline, and emails the invite to the buyer / seller.

Join or start a meeting

  • Click the Zoom link on the lead's timeline — Zoom opens in your browser or desktop client.
  • Any updates you make in Zoom (reschedule, cancel) are synced back to the lead automatically.

After the meeting

  • Immob24 attaches a short summary card to the lead once the meeting ends (start, end, participants).
  • If your plan has AI features enabled, you can generate a follow-up email from the summary in one click.

3. Removing the integration

From Immob24

  1. Open Settings → Integrations.
  2. On the Zoom tile, click Disconnect.
  3. Confirm. Existing Zoom links on past leads stay intact; no new meetings can be created until you reconnect.

From Zoom

  1. Sign in to the Zoom Marketplace at marketplace.zoom.us.
  2. Click Manage → Added Apps in the top-right.
  3. Find Immob24 and click Remove.
  4. Confirm the removal. Zoom immediately revokes Immob24's access to your account.
When you remove the app from either side, all authorization tokens are invalidated and Immob24 stops creating meetings on your Zoom account within seconds. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to previously exchanged data.

4. Troubleshooting

  • "Zoom said no" during Connect — usually your Zoom admin has restricted Marketplace apps. Ask your Zoom account owner to allow Immob24, or install it yourself if your account has permission.
  • Meeting doesn't appear in Zoom — refresh the Zoom desktop client or web page. If it still doesn't show, disconnect and reconnect from Settings → Integrations.
  • Duplicate invite emails — Zoom and Immob24 both send an invite by default. Turn off one under the meeting's Notifications settings.
  • Something else — email info@immob24.com with your account email and the meeting ID; we reply within one business day.

5. Data & privacy

Immob24 only stores what is needed to schedule and follow up on meetings you initiate: the Zoom meeting ID, join URL, start/end time, and participant list. We do not download recordings or transcripts. For full details see our Privacy Policy.
