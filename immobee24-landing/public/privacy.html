2. Data Protection Contact

For questions about data protection or to exercise your rights, please contact us at info@immob24.com.

3. Categories of personal data we process

  • Account data — name, email address, password hash, role

6. Retention

Account data is retained for the duration of the contract plus 6 months. Financial records are retained for 10 years as required by § 147 AO (German tax code).

7. Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. To exercise these rights, contact info@immob24.com.

8. Automated decision-making

We do not use fully automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). AI features provide suggestions only; a human always decides.

9. International transfers

Personal data is processed exclusively within the European Union (Frankfurt region). No transfers outside the EEA.

10. Right to complain

You may lodge a complaint with the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information: datenschutz-berlin.de.

11. Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced at least 30 days before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
